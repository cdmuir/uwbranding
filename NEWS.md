# uwbranding 0.1.0

* Initial release.
* `theme_uw()` and `theme_uw_dark()` provide ggplot2 themes using the UW–Madison brand identity.
* `scale_color_uw()`, `scale_fill_uw()`, `scale_color_uw_c()`, `scale_fill_uw_c()` provide discrete and continuous color scales.
* `scale_color_uw_dark()`, `scale_fill_uw_dark()`, `scale_color_uw_dark_c()`, `scale_fill_uw_dark_c()` provide scales optimised for dark backgrounds.
* `uw_color()` retrieves brand hex codes by name.
* `use_uw_pdf()`, `use_uw_revealjs()`, `use_uw_beamer()` copy Quarto templates into user projects.
