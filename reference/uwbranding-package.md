# uwbranding: UW-Madison Brand Identity for R and Quarto

Provides ggplot2 themes, color palettes, and Quarto document templates
conforming to the University of Wisconsin-Madison brand identity.
Includes light and dark variants for PDF documents, RevealJS
presentations, and Beamer slides. Quarto template files are copied into
user projects via use_uw_pdf(), use_uw_revealjs(), and use_uw_beamer().
